# Product

## Register

product

## Users

LIFE IN SYNC is built for one person managing daily life and focused practice on one iPhone. The user expects fast, trustworthy tools that work offline, preserve privacy, and stay out of the way while they complete real tasks.

## Product Purpose

LIFE IN SYNC is a native, local-first personal operating system. A shared Dashboard summarizes and routes, while each module owns its real workflows. Success means the user can plan, practice, record, and review without accounts, cloud dependence, or autonomous AI behavior.

Garage is the flagship practice module. It should help a golfer move quickly from intent to focused execution through serious, player-facing tools such as practice sessions, swing review, and Tempo Builder.

## Brand Personality

Calm, premium, practical.

The product should feel quietly confident, Apple-native, and tool-first. Copy is direct and useful. Visual atmosphere supports focus without becoming theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Generic demo-app styling or default ugly `Form` screens on flagship surfaces.
- Generic AI-app visuals, fake dashboard complexity, or autonomous-agent behavior.
- Clutter, heavy card stacks, card-inside-card layouts, and database-facing practice flows.
- Gimmicky labels, novelty sounds, playful or corny treatments, noisy gradients, excessive glow, and decorative theatrics.
- Cloud-first, social, collaborative, subscription, or account-driven product patterns.

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears into the task.** Make the next meaningful action obvious and remove competing controls.
2. **Modules own their workflows.** Preserve module boundaries and keep navigation inside the correct product layer.
3. **Premium means restraint.** Earn hierarchy through spacing, typography, surface depth, and precise interaction before decoration.
4. **Trust is visible.** AI remains advisory, user actions remain explicit, and weak evidence degrades honestly.
5. **Practice stays player-facing.** Garage execution screens prioritize goal, setup, cue, timing or tracking, and bottom actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Use native SwiftUI semantics and descriptive accessibility labels.
- Maintain at least 44pt touch targets and clear selected, disabled, and running states.
- Preserve readable contrast across dark flagship surfaces.
- Respect Reduce Motion and use motion only to communicate state.
- Support Dynamic Type and resilient layouts without hiding primary actions.
